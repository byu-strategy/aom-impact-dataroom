Our Mission

The wilderness doesn't ask what you did. It asks what you've got today.

Service builds bonds most people never experience, and leaving it can mean losing the team, the purpose, and the pace that held everything together. Last Frontier Corps puts those pieces back in reach: a crew, a plan, and real work to do, in some of the most demanding and beautiful country on earth.

We are not a clinic and we don't replace professional care. We're the trailhead: a place to reconnect, decompress, and find people worth calling when the trip is over.

Brotherhood & sisterhood

Small groups of veterans, led by veterans. Nobody has to explain themselves.

Purpose outdoors

Real objectives, real conditions, real skills. The kind of challenge that clears your head.

Connection that lasts

The trip ends; the crew doesn't. We help veterans stay connected to each other and to support.

Programs

Every season has a reason to get out.

Trips run year-round across Alaska's seasons. Experience isn't required: we bring the know-how and teach as we go.

Summer

Saltwater Fishing

Halibut, salmon, and rockfish on the open water of Alaska's coast.

Fall

Waterfowl Hunts

Early mornings in the marsh, decoys out, and the flight coming in at first light.

Seasonal

Big & Small Game Hunts

Hunts of all kinds, from day trips to backcountry camps, run safely and by the regs.

Winter

Ice Fishing

Augers, shanties, and long conversations over a hole in the ice.

Winter

Snow Machining

Covering big country fast across the frozen backcountry.

Summer

Camping

Nights under the midnight sun or the northern lights. Fire, gear, and good company.

Year-round

Sightseeing

Glaciers, wildlife, and the places that remind you how big the world is.

How It Works

From application to the trailhead.

  1. Apply. Tell us a little about your service and what kind of trip interests you.
  2. Connect. A member of our team reaches out to talk through fit, timing, and logistics.
  3. Go. Gear up and head out with a small group of fellow veterans.
  4. Stay in touch. Keep the connection going after you're back.
